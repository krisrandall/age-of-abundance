Work-life Balance for Busy Minds - Christine Parfitt

Episode 7 · 10 May 2021 · 1 h 2 min

Download the mp3

"I think the reason people conventionally started talking about work-life balance is because the amount of work that needs to be done, regardless as to if you are working for someone else in a job or working for yourself, far exceeds the number of hours available to do them."

Christine Parfitt, founder of Block & Table, talks with me and unpacks exactly what work-life balance means, and how to find it, especially if you have a busy mind that loves working on solving problems all the time.
