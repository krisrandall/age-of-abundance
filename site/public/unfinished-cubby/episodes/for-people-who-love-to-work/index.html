For people who love to work - Louise Flynn

Episode 4 · 19 April 2021 · 43 min

Download the mp3

Work/Life balance for people who love to work.

For some, the best way to build work/life balance is to build a business that focuses on helping the people who value you with products or service that play to your strengths.

Louise shares her story that is still a work-in-progress to reframe her work life to bring more satisfaction to my overall life. Where do you start and what resources can help you?

Louise's story is wonderful to listen to if you are thinking about making the leap into starting your own business.
