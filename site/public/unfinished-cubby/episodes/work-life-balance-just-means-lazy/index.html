In this very first episode of The Unfinished Cubby podcast, Kris talks with Jeff Mustard on the theme of "Work life balance just means lazy".

We explore meaning making and solid techniques for planning a good life in the context of family and work.

This episode was filmed on location in the cubby house that inspired the podcast.
