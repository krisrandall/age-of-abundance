Successful Intentional Communities - Jason Hilder

Episode 11 · 28 June 2021 · 1 h 13 min

Download the mp3

Intentionally formed communities are often the fertile seedbeds for holistic, regenerative solutions. What is the dream you would like to manifest?

In this engaging conversation Kris learns about the core principles that make for a successful intentional community.

His guest, Dr Jason Hilder, has freshly completed his PHD titled "We have lived together longer than the average Australian family: Understanding the Intentional Community Living Arrangements"

----------

Jason offers courses to help groups to go through the initial stages of community development, as well as community development advice and support for groups challenged with differences of opinion. Information can be found about this at www.jason.hilder.com.
