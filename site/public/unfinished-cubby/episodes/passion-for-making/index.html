Passion for Making - Stephanie Piper

Episode 10 · 21 June 2021 · 27 min

Download the mp3

Stephanie Piper is someone whose work is 100% a reflection of her life passion.

She runs the Maker Space for the University of Southern Queensland, and is a founder of the wearable electronic fashion startup Elkei.

Her success in pursuing her passion is inspiring.

This conversation is both interesting and invaluable for anyone who loves making.
